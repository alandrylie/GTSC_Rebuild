About Glenrothes Target Shooting Club (GTSC)

Originally founded in 1969 as a smallbore rifle club, GTSC has seen several developments over the years.

  • Fully approved by the Scottish Government / Home Office
  • Recognised by HMRC as a Community Amateur Sports Club (CASC)

Facilities

25-yard indoor shooting range, clubroom, kitchen, and toilet facilities.

Access: the range is on the first floor and is reached by one flight of stairs.

New Members

If you wish to join the club, please email to request an induction. There is currently no waiting list to join.

Existing Members

All members are reminded to attend an official club night to have their photograph taken for their ID card.

Visitors & Spectators

Non-members are not permitted entry unless prior arrangements have been made with the Club Secretary.

Spectators are not permitted within the club. An exception is made for a parent or guardian supervising a junior member.
