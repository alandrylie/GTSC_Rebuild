Before you apply

Induction

  • Our induction officer will text you to arrange an induction. You can see the club, have a chat and try 10m air rifles.
  • After the induction you can have up to 2 further visits before you need to join.

Probation

  • New members serve a probationary period of at least 3 months, with at least 50% attendance.
  • If you meet the requirements, the committee votes at its next meeting and you become a full member.

Keys

  • After a year as a full member you can apply for a key (£40 deposit) to attend outside official nights, on a first come, first served basis.

Firearms

  • Probationary members cannot use club rimfire rifles. Full members can.
  • If you hold your own Firearm Certificate, you can use your own firearms once you are a full member.
  • Some members have Section 1 firearms. These can only be held or shot by the certificate holder. No one else may use or touch them.
